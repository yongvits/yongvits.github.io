Noah’s Magic Door
Concept design / My piece is called “Noah’s Magic Door”, and it features characters from my own company, Mushroom Studio, where we normally focus on character design.

The idea was inspired by the story of Noah’s Ark and the concept of judgment day. I reimagined it in a modern, symbolic way—replacing the ark with a hat, and the animals with our original characters. The hat becomes a kind of vessel, helping these characters escape a massive flood.

At the top of the hat, there’s a magical doorway that opens into another world—a calm, safe place filled with sky and clouds. For me, it’s a visual story about survival, coming together, and holding onto hope during difficult times.
